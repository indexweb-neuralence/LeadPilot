PRICING FAQ

Questions about pricing?

Can I switch plans later?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time directly from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, while downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle.
How does annual billing work?
Annual billing is charged upfront for the entire year at a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. You will receive a single annual invoice.
What counts as a Lead Generator credit?
One credit allows you to export or reveal verified contact details (email address and direct phone line) for one lead. Unused credits rollover for active monthly and annual subscribers.
Is there a limit on users?
All LeadPilot plans scale per user/month. You can add as many team members as needed, and each user gets full access to the features included in your plan level.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. There are no long-term commitments or lock-in contracts for Starter and Growth plans. You can cancel anytime from your dashboard with one click.
What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
If you run out of credits, you can easily top up credit packs or upgrade to the next tier without interrupting your sales sequences or workflows.
Do you offer custom plans?
Yes! Our Enterprise plan is tailored for larger organisations requiring custom credit allocations, enterprise security, SLA guarantees, and custom integrations.
What is included in the free trial?
Our 14-day free trial gives you full access to Growth plan features so you can test lead generation, CRM pipelines, AI chatbot, meeting booking, and sales coaching risk-free.
