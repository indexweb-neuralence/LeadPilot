Meet the Client

Orbit Logistics operates ocean, air, and overland freight networks connecting manufacturers in Asia, Europe, and North America. With over 80 Account Managers across 12 regional offices, managing multi-currency contracts and complex freight tenders required tight pipeline coordination.

Before LeadPilot, each regional office maintained isolated CRM instances or custom spreadsheets, resulting in inaccurate executive forecasts and frequent deal delays.

❌ Quarterly revenue forecasts missed targets by up to 35% due to stale deals.
❌ Managers spent 6 hours per week chasing reps for manual deal stage updates.
🎯 Goal: Standardize pipeline visibility and automate deal stage progression tracking.

The Challenge

Supply chain sales cycles often span 3 to 9 months, involving multiple stakeholders including logistics managers, procurement directors, and CFOs. Without automated stage tracking, deals frequently sat in "Negotiation" for months without rep activity, skewing pipeline forecasts and hiding churn risks.

Executive leadership had zero real-time visibility into which deals were genuinely moving forward versus those that had stalled.

The LeadPilot Solution

Orbit Logistics unified all 12 regional teams under LeadPilot CRM & AI Pipeline Management:

  • 📦 Unified Multi-Currency CRM: Created real-time global deal views across USD, EUR, GBP, and AED.
  • 📦 AI Stale Deal Alerts: Automatically flagged deals without activity for >7 days, prompting rep follow-up.
  • 📦 Predictive Revenue Analytics: Weighted pipeline stages using historic win-rate data for 95%+ accurate forecasting.
