Meet the Client

Velocity Financial provides institutional treasury management, automated risk hedging, and commercial lending solutions to mid-market enterprises across North America and Europe.

Their sales team deals exclusively with C-suite financial executives (CFOs, Corporate Treasurers, Controllers) where trust, compliance, and highly personalized messaging are paramount.

❌ Cold outreach emails frequently landed in spam folders due to poor data accuracy.
❌ SDRs spent 3 hours per prospect crafting custom pre-meeting research briefs.
🎯 Goal: Achieve 99%+ email deliverability and scale C-suite meeting bookings.

The Challenge

Financial executives are among the most heavily targeted buyers in B2B. Generic bulk email cadences produce zero results, while manual 1-to-1 research limits outbound reps to fewer than 10 contacts per day.

Furthermore, financial data privacy regulations require absolute data accuracy and strict consent logging. Velocity's previous contact provider suffered from high bounce rates that compromised domain reputation.

The LeadPilot Solution

Velocity deployed LeadPilot verified data and AI sales assistant tools to empower their enterprise outbound team:

  • 🏦 Live Verification Engine: Every CFO and Treasurer email was verified live via SMTP checks before sequence enrollment.
  • 🏦 Multi-Channel AI Sequences: Automated multi-step email cadences combined with personalized LinkedIn touchpoints.
  • 🏦 AI Sales Assistant Briefings: Automatically summarized target company financial reports and news inside the CRM.
