01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that websites can store on your device when you visit them.

They can help websites remember information about your visit, maintain sessions, preserve preferences, understand website performance, and provide functionality.

Some cookies remain only for the duration of your browser session, while others may remain on your device for a specified period unless removed earlier.

LeadPilot may also use technologies that perform similar functions, where applicable.

02

How We Use Cookies

LeadPilot may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • •operate and secure our website;
  • •maintain user sessions;
  • •remember user preferences;
  • •support authentication;
  • •understand how visitors interact with our website;
  • •measure website and feature performance;
  • •identify and resolve technical issues;
  • •improve usability and user experience;
  • •understand which pages and features are useful;
  • •support marketing or attribution activities where permitted; and
  • •remember cookie and privacy choices.

The cookies actually deployed may vary as LeadPilot's website, services, and technology providers change.

04

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies support functions required for the website or service to operate securely and reliably.

These may support:

  • •authentication;
  • •account sessions;
  • •security;
  • •fraud or abuse prevention;
  • •network management;
  • •load balancing;
  • •saving privacy and cookie preferences; and
  • •essential website functionality.

Because these technologies are necessary to provide functionality requested by the user or maintain essential operation, they may not be subject to the same optional controls as analytics or marketing cookies, depending on applicable requirements.

Users may still be able to restrict cookies through their browser, although doing so can cause parts of LeadPilot to stop functioning correctly.

05

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may allow LeadPilot to remember choices or preferences that improve your experience.

These could include:

  • •interface preferences;
  • •language preferences;
  • •remembered settings;
  • •display preferences; and
  • •selected options.

Where functional cookies are treated as non-essential, they should respect the choices made through the cookie preference interface.

06

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors interact with LeadPilot.

Information collected through analytics may help us understand:

  • •which pages are visited;
  • •how visitors navigate the website;
  • •general engagement with features;
  • •website performance;
  • •technical errors;
  • •traffic patterns; and
  • •aggregate usage trends.

We use this information to understand and improve the performance, usability, and effectiveness of LeadPilot.

Where consent or another permission is required for analytics technologies, those technologies should operate according to the user's applicable cookie preferences.

07

Marketing Cookies

Where LeadPilot uses advertising, campaign measurement, attribution, or similar technologies, marketing cookies may be used to understand the effectiveness of campaigns or communications.

These technologies may help us understand how visitors arrive at LeadPilot or interact with marketing campaigns.

Where required, marketing cookies should remain disabled unless the user has made the appropriate choice.

10

How Long Do Cookies Last?

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods of time depending on their type:

SESSION COOKIES

Session cookies generally expire when the relevant browser session ends.

PERSISTENT COOKIES

Persistent cookies may remain for a defined period or until removed, depending on their purpose and configuration.

The exact duration of individual cookies may vary depending on the cookie and technology provider.

Browser Controls & Do Not Track

Restricting cookies through browser settings may affect certain website functionality.

Some browsers and devices may also transmit privacy preference signals such as “Do Not Track” or other recognised preference mechanisms.

The way LeadPilot responds to browser-level privacy signals may depend on applicable requirements and the technical capabilities of the website.
