Executive Summary

According to McKinsey Insights, the average B2B SDR ramp-up period requires 4.8 months before reaching 100% quota attainment. Manual call shadowing by managers covers less than 5% of total sales interactions.

By deploying LeadPilot AI Sales Coach, sales leaders analyze 100% of recorded discovery and demo calls, surfacing talk-to-listen imbalances, objection handling gaps, and top-performer audio snippets to reduce ramp-up time by 60%.

2. The Traditional SDR Onboarding Bottleneck

Traditional sales coaching relies on manual observation. Managers physically cannot shadow dozens of discovery calls weekly, leading to subjective feedback and uncorrected bad habits.

3. Automated Call Scoring & Scorecards

Every recorded call is evaluated against standardized MEDDPICC and value proposition scorecards automatically.

4. Optimizing Talk-to-Listen Ratios

Gong Labs research demonstrates that top account executives talk for less than 43% of a discovery call. LeadPilot triggers real-time alerts when reps enter 90-second uninterrupted monologues.

5. Live Playbook Recommendations & Snippet Libraries

New reps can self-serve top performer call snippets directly inside AI Sales Assistant to learn how seasoned AEs navigate security and pricing objections.

6. The 60-Day Enablement Blueprint

  1. Days 1–15: Self-serve listening to top 20 gold-standard call recordings.
  2. Days 16–30: AI-evaluated roleplay simulations in LeadPilot Coach.
  3. Days 31–60: Live call recording with real-time battlecards and automated rubric feedback.

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Top performers maintain a talk-to-listen ratio under 43%, letting buyers speak for 57% of the conversation.
