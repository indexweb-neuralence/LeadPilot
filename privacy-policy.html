Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LeadPilot (“LeadPilot”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, protects, and otherwise processes personal information when individuals and organisations access or use the LeadPilot website, applications, platform, products, and related services.

LeadPilot is a product of Neuralence Solutions FZCO, based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

By accessing or using LeadPilot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Who We Are

LeadPilot is an AI-powered sales and revenue platform developed and operated as a product of Neuralence Solutions FZCO.

LeadPilot provides tools that may include lead generation, customer relationship management (CRM), lead capture, meeting booking, quotations, sales sequences, AI-powered assistance, AI chatbots, sales coaching, pipeline management, analytics, and related sales operations functionality.

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, Neuralence Solutions FZCO may act as a controller of certain information collected directly through LeadPilot and, depending on the service and context, may process other information on behalf of LeadPilot customers.
