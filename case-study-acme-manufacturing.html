Meet the Client

Acme Manufacturing is a global leader in high-precision CNC machinery and automated industrial robotics. Headquartered in Chicago with distribution hubs across 14 countries, their sales team handles complex enterprise contracts ranging from $150,000 to $2.5M.

Before adopting LeadPilot, Acme's outbound sales reps relied on outdated trade directory databases, manual LinkedIn searches, and disconnected spreadsheets that delayed follow-ups and wasted hundreds of rep hours monthly.

❌ 42% of purchased lead emails bounced or went to unmonitored general inboxes.
❌ Account Executives spent 14 hours per week manually copying data into CRM.
🎯 Goal: Automate prospect discovery, verify contact info live, and double pipeline volume.

The Challenge

Acme Manufacturing faced a classic enterprise sales scaling bottleneck. While their engineering and manufacturing capabilities were world-class, their sales pipeline was throttled by manual administrative friction and inaccurate prospect data.

Sales reps spent nearly 40% of their workday cross-referencing contact info, manually logging email touchpoints, and attempting to reach out to buyers who had left target companies months prior. High bounce rates damaged domain deliverability, and leads took over 4 days to receive initial responses.

The LeadPilot Solution

Acme deployed LeadPilot across its 35-person outbound sales organization. By consolidating lead discovery, data verification, automated sequences, and CRM pipeline tracking into a single operating system, Acme transformed its sales motion:

  • 🔹 Lead Generator: Reps built targeted filters for VP of Operations and Plant Directors in North America with verified emails.
  • 🔹 Multi-Channel Sequences: Automated 14-day touchpoint cadences combining email and direct calendar booking.
  • 🔹 AI Sales Assistant: Automated pre-meeting company research briefings directly inside the CRM.
