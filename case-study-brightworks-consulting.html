Meet the Client

BrightWorks Consulting advises Fortune 1000 executives on digital transformation, operational efficiency, and M&A integration. Their partners and senior consultants manage high-touch advisory retainers starting at $75,000.

Because consulting leads are high-value and custom, senior partners previously spent up to 15 hours per proposal writing scope documents, gathering past project benchmarks, and updating CRM records.

❌ Proposals took an average of 6 days to generate and send to clients.
❌ Partners lost billable advisory hours on manual administrative tasks.
🎯 Goal: Automate proposal creation and streamline client meeting documentation.

The Challenge

In professional services, consultants are the product. Every hour spent manually formatting proposals or updating CRM fields is an hour taken away from billable client advisory work or high-level relationship building.

Slow proposal generation was costing BrightWorks deals: prospective clients frequently made decisions before BrightWorks could deliver custom multi-page scope documents.

The LeadPilot Solution

BrightWorks equipped their partners and practice leaders with LeadPilot AI Sales Assistant & Quotation tools:

  • ✨ Automated Meeting Intelligence: Transcribed discovery calls, extracted action items, and updated CRM deal notes automatically.
  • ✨ Smart Pipeline Tracking: Tracked proposal views, sign-offs, and payment milestones in real time.
