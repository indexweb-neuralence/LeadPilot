FAQ

Questions?
We’ve got answers.

Everything you need to know about LeadPilot AI Chatbot—from training and deployment to conversations, qualification, and what happens next.

+
How does LeadPilot AI Chatbot work?
LeadPilot AI Chatbot learns from your business knowledge and uses it to answer visitor questions in real time. It can understand intent, qualify potential customers, and move conversations into the next step of your sales workflow.
+
What can I train the AI Chatbot on?
You can connect your website content, FAQs, product information, pricing, policies, business documents, and other knowledge sources. LeadPilot uses this information to understand your business and provide relevant answers to visitors.
+
How does LeadPilot know what to answer?
LeadPilot searches the business knowledge you’ve connected and uses the most relevant information to generate its response. This helps the chatbot answer based on your actual business content rather than giving generic responses.
+
Can the AI Chatbot qualify leads automatically?
Yes. LeadPilot can understand visitor intent during conversations and identify high-intent prospects. It can capture relevant information and turn qualified conversations into opportunities for your sales team.
+
Can it book meetings directly from a conversation?
Yes. When a visitor is ready to speak with your team, LeadPilot can move the conversation toward meeting booking so qualified prospects can take the next step without leaving the experience.
+
What happens after a lead is qualified?
The conversation doesn’t stop at qualification. LeadPilot can continue the workflow from intent detection to meeting booking, quotation generation, AI-powered follow-ups, pipeline updates, and ultimately help move the opportunity toward closing.
+
Can I customize the chatbot to match my brand?
Yes. You can customize the chatbot experience to better match your website and brand, including how the chatbot appears and how visitors interact with it.
+
How do I add LeadPilot AI Chatbot to my website?
LeadPilot provides simple deployment options that let you add the chatbot to your website without rebuilding your site. Once configured, the chatbot can start engaging visitors directly on your website.
+
Does it connect with the rest of my LeadPilot sales workflow?
Yes. That’s one of the key advantages of LeadPilot. A chatbot conversation can become part of the same connected sales workflow—from AI Chatbot → Intent Detected → Meeting Booked → Quote Generated → AI-powered Follow-Up → Pipeline Updated → Deal Closed.
+
Is my business and customer data secure?
LeadPilot is designed with security and privacy in mind. Your connected business knowledge and customer conversations are handled within the platform so your team can use AI while maintaining control over your business information.
