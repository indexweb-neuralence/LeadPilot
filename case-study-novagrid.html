Meet the Client

NovaGrid Cloud provides high-performance cloud orchestration software for engineering teams. Processing over 40,000 inbound website visits per month, NovaGrid required an intelligent automated qualification system capable of separating tier-1 enterprise prospects from casual open-source users.

Their growth team faced a classic high-volume SaaS challenge: Account Executives were overwhelmed by unqualified leads while high-value enterprise buyers waited hours for a response.

❌ Inbound leads waited an average of 48 minutes for sales follow-up.
❌ Account Executives spent 50% of demo calls on unqualified leads.
🎯 Goal: Automate real-time qualification and instant calendar booking.

The Challenge

In modern B2B SaaS, speed to lead is everything. Research proves that contacting a prospect within 5 minutes increases conversion odds by 9× compared to 30 minutes. NovaGrid's legacy form stack submitted leads into a general queue where SDRs manually evaluated company size and revenue metrics before sending a calendar link.

This manual latency led to drop-offs, missed demo opportunities, and frustrated buyers who ended up booking calls with competitors.

The LeadPilot Solution

NovaGrid deployed LeadPilot AI Lead Forms & AI Chatbot directly on their product pages and pricing portal:

  • ⚡ AI Chatbot Qualification: Engaged website visitors instantly, asking 3 dynamic qualification questions based on tech stack and team size.
  • ⚡ Instant Round-Robin Booking: Qualified buyers with >50 engineering seats were offered immediate booking with dedicated AEs based on time zone.
  • ⚡ CRM Pipeline Sync: Lead profile, tech stack data, and meeting notes synced to LeadPilot CRM in under 2 seconds.
