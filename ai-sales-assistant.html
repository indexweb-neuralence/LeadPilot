FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about LeadPilot AI Sales Assistant and how it helps your sales team turn sales data into clear, actionable insights.

+
How does LeadPilot AI Sales Assistant work?
LeadPilot AI Sales Assistant analyzes the sales information available across your LeadPilot workspace—including leads, deals, pipeline activity, meetings, quotations, follow-ups, revenue, and team performance. Ask a question in plain English and the assistant turns that context into clear answers, insights, summaries, risks, and recommended next actions.
+
What can I ask the AI Sales Assistant?
Ask about almost any part of your sales operation. For example: "Which leads need attention today?", "Show me deals at risk", "Give me this week's team performance summary", or "What's our current pipeline revenue?" The assistant can surface insights across leads, deals, meetings, quotations, follow-ups, conversions, revenue, forecasting, and team performance.
+
Does it understand my CRM and sales pipeline?
Yes. The AI Sales Assistant uses the sales context available within LeadPilot, including lead activity, deal stages, meetings, quotations, follow-ups, pipeline movement, and related sales information. This allows it to provide answers based on your actual sales activity rather than generic responses.
+
Can it identify leads and deals that need attention?
Yes. LeadPilot can surface opportunities that may require attention, such as leads waiting for follow-up, deals with slowing activity, pending quotations, or opportunities showing signs of stalling. It can then provide the relevant context and recommend what your team should focus on next.
+
Can it analyze team performance?
Yes. You can ask for sales performance summaries using the information available in LeadPilot, including leads, deals, conversions, pipeline activity, and revenue. Managers can quickly understand how the team is performing without manually reviewing multiple reports.
+
Can it help with revenue forecasting?
LeadPilot can analyze available pipeline and deal information to help your team understand expected revenue, opportunities in progress, potential risks, and changes across the sales pipeline. Instead of digging through dashboards, you can ask forecasting questions directly in natural language.
+
Does it recommend what to do next?
Yes. The AI Sales Assistant goes beyond simply presenting information. Based on the sales context it analyzes, it can surface relevant next-step recommendations—such as which leads need follow-up or which opportunities may require immediate attention.
+
Does it use the latest sales activity?
The AI Assistant should use the latest information available within LeadPilot when generating new answers and insights, so changes across leads, deals, meetings, quotations, follow-ups, and other sales activity can be reflected in its responses.
+
Can my entire sales team use it?
LeadPilot AI Sales Assistant is designed to support different sales workflows. Sales representatives can use it to understand leads, deals, and next actions, while managers can use it for pipeline visibility, revenue insights, forecasting, and team performance analysis.
+
Is my business data secure?
LeadPilot should handle sales and customer information according to the security, privacy, access-control, and data-handling standards implemented by the LeadPilot platform.
